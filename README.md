# AI Email Reply Assistant — Interactive Demo

A customer email arrives. The AI drafts the reply in seconds — and books the visit.

## What it shows

You play the owner of fictional ClearFlow Plumbing Co. At 7:42 AM an email lands from a homeowner with a leaking water heater. You open it, the AI drafts a reply (with detected-intent chips and three tone options — Friendly & reassuring, Professional, Concise), you edit it if you like, and send it. The customer confirms the same-day 2:00 PM visit, and the appointment is booked — with a live seconds timer showing how fast it happened.

## Honest framing

This is a **scripted simulation**. No real emails are sent, no real bookings are made, and all names, addresses, and businesses are fictional.
